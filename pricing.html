How paying works

What is a memory link?
A short link the AI writes between a word and its meaning. You see it in full the first time. It fades as you recall the word, and then it retires.
Where do I pay?
On the web, in Moreably's Plans page. Payments are handled by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record.
What currency, and is tax included?
US dollars. Tax is added where it applies, and you see the total before you pay.
Does my plan work on my phone?
Yes. Your plan works in the app on Android and on the web. Plans and packs are bought on the web.
How do I cancel?
In the app on the web, Settings → Manage plan opens Paddle's customer portal: receipts, payment method and cancelling.
What happens to links I do not use?
Each month starts with your plan's links; unused ones do not carry over. Pack links stay until you use them.
