Paying

Payments are handled by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. Paddle's buyer terms apply to every payment, and nothing here takes away a right they or the law where you live give you.

Plans

Plus and Pro are subscriptions, charged each month or each year until you cancel. You cancel in the app on the web: Settings → Manage plan opens Paddle's customer portal. A cancelled plan stays active to the end of the period you paid for, then your account is on the Free plan. Cancelling does not refund the current period.

Extra memory links (packs)

A pack is one payment, not a subscription. Extra memory links are used only after your plan's memory links for the month are used. They do not expire. Deleting your account deletes them, with no refund. If you want a refund for a pack, ask Paddle before you delete your account.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Paddle under its buyer terms. In the EU, the EEA and the UK you can withdraw within 14 days of a payment, unless you have started to use what you bought.
